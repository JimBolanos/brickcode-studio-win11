nbc.exe - Next Byte Codes compiler
==================================

nbc (Next Byte Codes) compiles NXC and NBC programs for the LEGO MINDSTORMS NXT.
It is part of BricxCC, by John Hansen.

  Version bundled : nbc 1.2.1.r5, from the BricxCC test release 2011-10-24
                    (supports the enhanced NXT firmware 1.31)
